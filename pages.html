offline

download the whole website

play every game with no internet. on windows or mac, download the .zip, unzip it and open the launcher inside. on a chromebook, press the button to save the site in chrome.

windowsunzip, then double-click Play Offline (Windows).batmacunzip, then right-click Play Offline (Mac).command → open

windows / mac: the launcher starts a tiny web server on your own computer (nothing is uploaded) and opens the site in your browser at http://localhost:8642. keep its window open while you play. saves stay in your browser, separate from the online site.

chromebook: chromebooks can't run the launcher, so press the chromebook button instead and wait for it to finish (about 40 MB). after that, missing.website/pages.html and every game open in chrome even with no internet. to get an app icon too, open chrome's ⋮ menu → cast, save and share → install page as app. works the same in chrome on windows and mac.
