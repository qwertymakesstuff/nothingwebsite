# Internet History (alternate timeline)

The internet of FAKEOS has its own companies and events. Everything here is canon; content must agree with it.

## Early web (1994–2004)
- **1999** — Maren Okafor and Del Hutchins launch **Findr** above a bike shop in Palo Verde, CA. Ranking system: TrailRank (pages ranked by who links to them).
- **2002** — Findr adds "Lucky Dip". Del insists it's a joke.
- **2003** — **MyPlace** social network launches: profile songs, glitter cursors, the Top 8.
- **2003–2004** — **Gabber** instant messenger is everywhere; the door-creak sign-in sound; away messages.
- **2004** — Findr Mail launches with 1 GB.

## The middle years (2005–2009)
- **2005** — Ruth Ambrose and "Kel" start **Threadit** in Ruth's Portland basement. The first thread, "test", gets a fourth reply from an account that is deleted the same day: "hello, I've been waiting."
- **April 2006** — **Vidder** launched by Teo Halvorsen and two university friends.
- **March 2007** — The **Lantern Tape**: account "lanternhouse" uploads one ~11-minute hallway video; removed in April. Never verified since.
- **2008** — Findr buys Vidder. Gabber changes its sign-in sound (the original is later "found" in 2011). **Blink** is founded by Corwin Vance (Feb 2008).
- **2008** — c/lostmedia founded (Oct). Wexley News 9 joins Vidder.
- **2009** — Dennis Pruitt (Pruitt Reviews) and Max Dolan (MaxPlaysStuff) start their channels.

## The Vidder era (2010–2013)
- **June 2010** — Pruitt's "Do Not Buy This Toaster" (Breadmaster Duo). Vidder replaces 5-star ratings with likes/dislikes the same year.
- **2 Oct 2010** — Walt Haskins uploads "it's for later" (19 seconds). Becomes the Gravy Boat Guy meme. Feb 2011 "clarification": he owns forty-one gravy boats.
- **2011** — "TOASTED" remix of Pruitt (Sept). Doug the cat vs the vacuum (Nov). Findr Circles social network closes after eleven months. MyPlace deletes all inactive profiles on 24 hours' notice (Nov 1).
- **4 Mar 2012** — The **Great Vidder Outage**: nine hours down, many 2011 comments lost ("a small number of comments were affected").
- **Sept 2012** — Blink forces **Timeline**. #GiveUsBackTheWall.
- **15 Mar 2013** — Gabber shuts down; users told to move to Blink Chat.
- **10 Jun 2013** — "The new Vidder" redesign video; 380k dislikes, ukulele.
- **Aug 2013** — **Volt Rush scandal**: leaked emails show Max Dolan was paid $85,000 and told to title his video "not sponsored". Apology Aug 21; he donates the fee.
- **Sept 2013** — GlitterGrrl x Lumiere palette (Sept 3). **Eastside power cut** in Port Wexley (Sept 19–20): a hum near the Mill Road substation; power returns at about 4:17 am; the fault "cleared itself".
- **Oct 2013** — Kayla's "about bree." (Oct 5), Bree's "my response." (Oct 7). RigDoctor tears down the Nikko SmartToast (camera pointed at the bread; phones home every 17 minutes). Max's Cragmoor Ep. 47 shows a door that has no open animation. hollowfrequency's "the hallway (restored)" surfaces.
- **14 Oct 2013** — The player's computer is switched on. A Threadit account, q_mardell, is created the same day and asks what the date is.

## Places
